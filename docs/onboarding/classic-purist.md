# Onboarding — the classic/purist pack

**Fourth pack (D223 order). Voice spec — ships when its pack JSON lands.**
Voice: minimal. The board, the notation, the record. The people are still
there — the pack just refuses to decorate them.

## Who you are

You are the **player** — the hand over White. No title, no court, no
management fiction laid over the pieces. The pieces keep their psychology and
their right to refuse; the frame around them is chess, not HR.

## What the pieces call things

| In the world | You know it as |
|---|---|
| the set | your army |
| a forfeit | a piece leaves the board |
| a forced move | an override |
| a hollow move | quiet quitting |
| the score sheet | the campaign's closing ledger |
| resignation en masse | the rout |
| forfeiture | dismissal — the arbiter ends your seat |
| a draw offered by fortune | grace — unearned, unclaimed |
| a declined invitation | a free agent passes |

## The voice

Compressed to the notation itself. A purist piece speaks rarely and
structurally — it cites the move, not the mood. The cause-legibility rule still
holds (every departure names its trigger), but the lines are spare:

> *"After {san}, the file is not worth holding. I resign the square."*

> *"The order stands. The reason for following it does not."*

## What to listen for

- **The record as voice.** In this pack the audit prose and the Judgement Seat
  carry more of the narrative weight than the dialogue — deliberately. Watch
  the fold, not the lines.
- **No cushion.** Without the management fiction, refusals land colder. There
  is no "Commander" softening the address; a piece that says no says it to you.
- **The chess underneath.** Purist is the pack for players who want the
  leadership dynamics priced but not staged — same system, bare frame.
