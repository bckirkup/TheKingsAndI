# Onboarding — the corporate pack

**Pilot-cohort pack (D223 order). Voice spec — ships when its pack JSON lands.**
Voice: org charts, performance reviews, the org, the board (of directors), the floor.

## Who you are

You are the **manager** — appointed by the org, reviewed by the board. Your
career is three roles; attrit the team badly enough and the board seats
someone else. The pieces call you nothing to your face. That's the pack's point:
in corporate, everything important is said politely.

## What the pieces call things

| In the world | You know it as |
|---|---|
| the team | your army |
| a resignation | a piece leaves mid-quarter |
| a directed outcome | an override — HR language for force |
| quiet quitting | literally quiet quitting — the pack's home idiom |
| the annual review | the campaign's closing ledger |
| a mass exodus | the rout — the team quits in waves |
| a restructure | dismissal — the board reassigns you |
| unexpected severance relief | grace — unearned, not your credit |
| an offer declined | a free agent reads Glassdoor and passes |

## The voice

Polished, careful, devastating in the passive voice. A corporate piece does not
rage-quit — it gives notice with a smile and names the exact decision in the
exit interview. Expect lines like:

> *"I want to flag a concern with the direction before I act on it, and I want it on the record that I flagged it."*

> *"Happy to take that ticket. Just noting this is the third late re-prioritization this sprint."*

## What to listen for

- **Polite litigants.** Every refusal sounds like a reasonable person
  documenting a disagreement. The grievance is never atmospheric — it is
  filed.
- **The reorg reading.** Dismissal here is a restructure announcement. "We're
  excited about the team's next chapter" means the same two endings: they
  shipped better without you, or the org was already hollow.
- **Quiet quitting's home turf.** The compliance-without-conviction register is
  where this pack is sharpest — detectable in the record, never announced.
