# Facilitator kit

The static kit for running *The Kings and I* as a leadership seminar. Everything
here is committed text — no runtime generation, no keys, no accounts (ADR 0004).
The model facilitator (D221) is out of scope for this directory; these scripts
are for the human in the room.

| Document | Use |
|---|---|
| `opening-script.md` | Read-aloud script for seating a cohort — sets the frame, the rules, and the one promise (the pieces talk back). |
| `closing-script.md` | Read-aloud script for the campaign debrief — Judgement Seat, the two hard endings, certificates. |
| `debrief-template.md` | Per-player one-pager. Mustache-style `{placeholders}` resolved from the debrief fold and certificate bundle. |
| `cohort-narrative-template.md` | The room-level readout — a fold over all players' journals and transcripts, written as one narrative. |

## What a facilitator needs to know before the first match

1. **Pieces are people in the fiction, instruments in the system.** They hold
   trust in two channels — your judgment (ability credence) and your care
   (benevolence credence). Both are visible in what they say; neither is a
   gauge you should promise to show.
2. **Every departure names a cause** (ADR 0018). When a piece refuses or walks,
   the line it speaks cites something the player did — a forced override, a
   spent comrade, a hope that ran out. If it ever doesn't, that's a bug, not a
   feature.
3. **The game never discloses strategy** (D28). A piece will say "you left me on
   that file." It will never say "you should have castled." Redirect chess
   questions to the audit's board-quality numbers, not to the pieces.
4. **The endings that teach are not the checkmates.** Dismissal has two
   readings — the successor outplays you (*you lost the room*) or fails too
   (*you broke the roster*, the worse one). Rout is the army choosing loss over
   service. Both are seminar gold; checkmate is just chess.
5. **Commendations are behavioural, never dispositional** (D90). Awards name
   what the record shows the player did, never what kind of person they are.
   Read citations, not vibes.

## Running order (pilot cohort, corporate pack)

1. Opening script (~10 min).
2. Players run a career — three appointments, dismissal risk between matches.
3. Closing script (~15 min) over the Judgement Seat fold.
4. Hand each player their debrief one-pager and certificate bundle.
5. Facilitator writes the cohort narrative offline from journals + transcripts.
