# Closing script (read-aloud, ~15 minutes, at the Judgement Seat)

Run after the last career concludes. Players should be looking at their own
debrief — Judgement Seat, the fold table, the epilogue — while you read.

---

Put the chess down for a minute. The checkmates were never the subject.

Look at your debrief. Not the result column — the fold. That's the part of the
record the pieces wrote. Your win score came from the board. Your Judgement
Seat index came from them: the trust they ended with, the harm that wasn't
bought with a point, the plies they spent complying without conviction, the
chairs they emptied.

Read your epilogue. Every career here ended one of a few ways. If yours says
*Victory* — look at what it actually says. The army outran your orders. They
became better than the hand that led them, and the record says so plainly,
because that's the only victory this game certifies. If yours says *Dismissed*,
read which dismissal you got. Did the King do better with your roster than you
did? Then you lost the room — the army was fine, and you were the problem. Did
he fail too? Then you broke the roster — you left a room nobody could lead.
The second is worse. Sit with whichever you earned.

If yours says *Rout*, understand what a rout means here: the desertion wasn't
cowardice, it was arithmetic. Each piece weighed staying against walking, and
walked. The cascade wasn't a mood — it was your management style, priced. And
if yours says *The campaign continues* — congratulations, you're still being
evaluated, and your next King has already heard of you.

Now the commendations. Every award on your page names a behavior, not a
personality. *Nobody drowned* means no career ended in burnout under your hand.
*The honest sacrifice* means a piece spent itself knowingly for the win and the
record kept its name. *The repaired breach* means someone who stopped believing
came back — which is the hardest thing this simulation measures, because
rupture is cheap to cause and ruinous to mend.

If you earned none of them, that's also a finding. The certificates are
replay-verifiable: anyone can take your seed and watch the whole career again.
There is no forgetting in this room.

One question to carry out of here, per player, and it's the only question the
game was ever asking:

**Your orders were followed because the pieces could see the board, or because
they trusted you — and do you know which it was?**

That's the seminar. The boards reset themselves. Thank you.
