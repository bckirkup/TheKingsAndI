# ADR 0046 — Release before resignation

- **Status:** deferred / unimplemented
- **Date:** 2026-08-01

## Decision

Immediately before conceding, the leader may eventually dismiss the roster
instead of spending it. This would convert desertion (“they left; you were
abandoned”) into release (“you spared them”). A release should pay
`τ_benev` and cost standing/mandate, be irreversible, and be prematurely
costly: releasing from a drawn or winning board reads as abandonment and costs
benevolence rather than buying it.

This remains unimplemented. It depends on pivotality and the impending-loss
shadow in ADR 0045 being real, because those terms establish why the timing and
value of sparing the roster matter.
